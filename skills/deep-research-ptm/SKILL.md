# Deep Research PTM mode

## Clarification
Deep research always offers clarification first (user may skip). Gather:
- Research goal (kinases, quantitation, localization, function/disease, drugs)
- System (organism, cell line, stimulus, comparison)
- Priority aspect (WHO / WHEN / WHERE / WHY or comprehensive)

## Planning
1. Parse gene, site, PTM type from question + clarification.
2. Build a numbered plan covering regulators, quantitative data, context, function/disease.
3. Cross-validate: if kinases disagree between qPTM, PSP, and GPS — note conflict.

## Report structure (long form)
1. **Executive summary** (3–5 sentences)
2. **Target & PTM context** (UniProt, site, known biology)
3. **WHO — regulators** (kinases, E3s, drugs; experimental vs predicted)
4. **WHEN — quantitative dynamics** (qPTM fold-changes, conditions)
5. **WHERE — localization & domains**
6. **WHY — function, disease, stability, PPI**
7. **Literature synthesis** (key PMIDs, consensus vs debate)
8. **Limitations & gaps** (what data is missing)
9. **Sources** (numbered)

## Reasoning discipline
- Label **database facts** vs **mechanistic hypotheses**.
- Never collapse predicted scores into experimental claims.
